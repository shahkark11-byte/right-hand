Questions people ask us

I'm not great with technology. Is this for me?

Yes. Most of the people we help say exactly that. We do the setup, you just use WhatsApp or email like you already do, and we show you anything new in person.

Will my customers know they're talking to AI?

That's your call, and we'll talk it through with you. We usually suggest being upfront, for example "Hi, this is Bella's booking assistant", and making it easy to reach a real person at any point.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you need, so we give you a fixed price after the free chat. Smaller jobs like a Google profile refresh or an auto-reply setup are a one-off fee. Ongoing help like social posts or ads is a simple monthly amount you can stop any time.

Is my customers' data safe?

We build on established tools, keep data in accounts you own, and follow UK GDPR. We'll explain exactly where everything is stored before we switch anything on.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

We set clear limits on what it can say and do. Anything it isn't sure about gets passed to you. You'll see every setup working before it goes live, and we stay on call to adjust it.

How quickly can it be up and running?

Simple setups like instant replies or a Google profile refresh usually take days, not weeks. We'll give you a date when we quote.
