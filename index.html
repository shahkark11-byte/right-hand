What the walk-through looks at

Four numbers show where a salon is losing customers.

  1. 1 · They find youHow many new people find you each week, on Instagram, Google or walking past?
  2. 2 · They get in touchHow many of them message or ring to ask?
  3. 3 · They book or buyHow many book and actually turn up?
  4. 4 · They come backHow many come back within two months?

The weakest of the four is where your money leaks, so that's where I start. Speeding up anything else first changes very little. Don't know one of the numbers? Then step one is counting it, so we can both see the difference later.

Questions people ask

I already use ChatGPT. Isn't that enough?

ChatGPT is great when you sit down and ask it something. What I set up works without you: it answers customers at midnight, sends the reminder, chases the invoice and posts the update while you're busy doing the actual work.

Do I need new apps or a new phone?

No. It runs on the phone you already have and the apps you already use, like WhatsApp, Gmail and Instagram. Anything new, I set up and show you in person.

I'm not great with technology. Is this for me?

Yes. Most owners I talk to say exactly that. I do the setup, you keep using your phone like you already do, and I show you anything new in person.

Do you do videos and social media too?

Yes. Send me the clips from your phone and I edit them into Reels, TikToks, Shorts or ads. I can also run your posting, your Google profile and local ads, so the people your AI is answering keep coming in.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you need, so you get one fixed price after the free walk-through. Smaller jobs, like a Google profile refresh or auto-replies, are a one-off fee. Ongoing help is a simple monthly amount you can stop any time.

Will I be stuck needing you?

No. Everything I build lives in your own accounts and keeps running if you stop paying me. You'd only pay the tools' own monthly fees, and I show you those before we start.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

I set clear limits on what it can say and do. Anything it isn't sure about comes to you. And I stay on call to adjust it.

Is my customers' data safe?

I build on established tools, keep data in accounts you own, and follow UK GDPR. I'll explain exactly where everything is stored before anything is switched on.
