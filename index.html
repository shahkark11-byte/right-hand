Questions people ask

I already use ChatGPT. Isn't that enough?

ChatGPT is great when you sit down and ask it something. What I set up works without you: it answers customers at midnight, sends the reminder, chases the invoice and posts the update while you're busy doing the actual work.

I'm not great with technology. Is this for me?

Yes. Most people I help say exactly that. I do the setup, you keep using WhatsApp and email like you already do, and I show you anything new in person.

Will it sound like a robot to my customers?

It's set up to talk like you: your greetings, your prices, your way of saying things. You'll read its replies before it goes live. I also suggest being upfront that it's an assistant and making it easy to reach you, which customers tend to appreciate.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you need, so you get a fixed price after the free chat. Smaller jobs like a Google profile refresh or auto-replies are a one-off fee. Ongoing help like social posts or ads is a simple monthly amount you can stop any time.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

I set clear limits on what it can say and do. Anything it isn't sure about comes to you. And I stay on call to adjust it.

Is my customers' data safe?

I build on established tools, keep data in accounts you own, and follow UK GDPR. I'll explain exactly where everything is stored before anything is switched on.
