Commercial Law, week 6 (my notes, EXAMPLE)
LO1 Types of business
- sole trader: no separate legal personality, unlimited liability
- partnership: in Scotland a firm is a legal person, Partnership Act 1890, s4(2)
- LLP needs 2 or more members, Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2000, s2(1)(a)
LO2 Companies
- Salomon v A Salomon & Co Ltd [1987] AC 22: company is separate from its members
- a private company needs at least one director, Companies Act 2006, s154(1)
- directors' duties, Companies Act 2006, ss171-177
LO3 Liability for advice
- Hedley Byrne & Co Ltd v Heller & Partners Ltd [1964] AC 465: careless statements relied on
- Smith v Jones 2019: something about AI advice?? check
