THE SHINWARI WAY

Lamb, salt, charcoal and time.

Shinwari cooking is famous for simple, meat-first dishes. Karahi, BBQ lamb, chappal kebab. Good meat, the right spices and a fire that is hot enough. There is nothing to hide behind, so we do not try.

Everything is cooked fresh to order, so it comes to your table when it is ready. Guests are often welcomed with kehwa, our green tea, while they wait. And the owners come to the table to say hello.
